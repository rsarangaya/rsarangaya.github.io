Wake My Stop

Android app

Location alarm for transit riders

Never miss your stop again. Pick any destination on a map, compare transit or driving routes, then start a trip. Your phone rings when you're a few minutes, a set distance, or a number of stops away, using GPS together with live ETAs. Favorites, recents, Home and Work shortcuts, and light/dark themes.
